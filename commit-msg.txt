[SEC-08] Fix: No rate limiting on login and registration endpoints

Found that the login endpoint had absolutely no protection against
repeated failed attempts. Ran 10 rapid login requests with a wrong
password back to back and every single one was processed instantly
with no delay, no lockout, and no blocking of any kind, meaning an
attacker could brute force passwords automatically at full speed
with no resistance at all. Registration had the same lack of any
throttling.

Fixed by installing express-rate-limit and creating three separate
limiters. A strict limiter on login allowing only 5 attempts per IP
every 15 minutes, since this is the most sensitive and most likely
to be targeted endpoint. A slightly looser limiter on registration
allowing 10 attempts per hour, since legitimate signups can
sometimes come in small bursts from shared networks. And a general
limiter applied globally across the entire app as a baseline, so
every endpoint has at least some protection against abuse, not just
authentication routes.

Made the same mistake as SEC-03 initially, where I added the new
rate limited routes but forgot to remove the old duplicate
unprotected route definitions above them, so Express kept matching
the old unprotected ones first. Fixed by removing the duplicates.

Retested after the fix by running the same 10 rapid login attempts
again, and confirmed the first 5 attempts return the normal
unauthorized response, while attempts 6 through 10 are correctly
blocked with a too many requests response instead.
