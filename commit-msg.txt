Add account lockout after repeated failed login attempts

Implemented per-account lockout as a second, independent layer of
brute force protection alongside the existing IP based rate
limiting from SEC-08. Rate limiting alone only protects against
fast automated attacks from a single IP, but does not stop an
attacker who spaces out attempts or distributes them across
multiple IP addresses to stay under the rate limit threshold.

Added failedLoginAttempts and lockedUntil fields to the user
schema. Each failed login attempt increments the counter for that
specific account. After 5 failed attempts, the account is locked
for 15 minutes regardless of which IP the requests come from,
returning a clear locked message with the remaining time. A
successful login resets both the failed attempt counter and any
active lock.

This works together with the existing rate limiter as layered
defense in depth, rate limiting blocks fast bursts immediately,
while account lockout protects against slower or distributed brute
force attempts that might otherwise stay under the rate limiting
threshold.
